# About Sattvik Bhojan

Sattvik Bhojan is a small cloud kitchen founded and primarily funded by **Nerraj Bharti**. The kitchen will be located in Dr. Gurumukh Singh Colony, Mandi Gobindgarh, Punjab.

Sattvik Bhojan will be operated by Dinesh, Promila Devi, and Mehak, under the guidance and support of Nerraj Bharti.

The purpose of Sattvik Bhojan is to provide fresh, simple, hygienic, and home-style food to customers through a convenient cloud-kitchen model.
